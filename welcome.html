PeekDrive is installed

Three quick steps and you are searching inside your files.

1

Pin PeekDrive

Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to PeekDrive: its icon stays one click away.

2

Open Google Drive and sign in

Click the PeekDrive icon, or the PeekDrive button next to Drive’s search bar, then “Sign in with Google”. PeekDrive only asks for read-only access: it can never modify your files.

While Google finishes its review of PeekDrive, it shows a warning screen when you sign in. This is expected: the access requested stays read-only.

3

Run your first search

Type a word: each result shows the passage, and “Open” takes you right to it. On Google Drive you can also press Cmd+Shift+F (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+F (Windows).
