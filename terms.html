The service

PeekDrive is a browser extension that searches inside the files of your Google Drive, shows the matching passages and opens a file at the right spot. It requires a Google account and your authorization to read your Drive (read-only access). By installing or using PeekDrive, you accept these terms.

Your Google account and your data

PeekDrive only reads your files, to search them, and cannot modify, move, share or delete anything in your Drive. Your files and searches only travel between your browser and Google: PeekDrive has no server. You can remove this access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions and uninstall the extension.
