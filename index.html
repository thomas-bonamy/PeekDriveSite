Find the passage, not just the file

PeekDrive adds to Google Drive what its search does not show: where your word is inside each file.

  • Search inside the content

    Type a word or a phrase: PeekDrive looks for it in the text of your documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs.

  • Passages in their context

    For each file, you see the text around your word, highlighted, for every occurrence in the file. You know whether it is the right one before opening anything.

  • One click to the right spot

    “Open” takes you to the right occurrence in a document, the right tab, row and column in a spreadsheet, the right slide in a presentation, the right passage in a PDF or a CSV.

  • The files you use every day

    Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, PDF, Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint, CSV and plain text. In My Drive and in shared drives.

  • Four ways in

    The PeekDrive icon in Chrome’s toolbar, the PeekDrive button next to Drive’s search bar, a line under Drive’s search suggestions, or the shortcut Cmd+Shift+F (Mac) / Ctrl+Shift+F (Windows) on Google Drive.

  • Private by design

    Read-only access, everything processed in your browser, no PeekDrive server, no AI. Your files only travel between your browser and Google.

How it works

Three steps, from Google Drive.

  1. 1

    Open PeekDrive

    Click its icon in Chrome’s toolbar or its button next to Drive’s search bar, then sign in with Google.

  2. 2

    Type a word

    For each file that contains it, PeekDrive shows the passages around your word, highlighted.

  3. 3

    Open at the right spot

    “Open” takes you to the passage. The editor’s own Find box stays open on your word, to go to the next or previous occurrence, and the word is copied to your clipboard.

Why PeekDrive asks for access to your Google Drive

PeekDrive asks you to sign in with Google and grant read-only access to your Google Drive, so it can search the text inside your files and show you the matching passages. It never modifies your files.

Who is it for?

For anyone who keeps their work in Google Drive and often looks for a precise passage. A few everyday uses:

  • Students

    Find where a concept is defined in your course notes, before an exam.

  • Lawyers

    Locate a clause in your contracts and open each one at the right paragraph.

  • Human resources

    Find a name in your tracking spreadsheets, down to the right row.

  • Researchers

    Find a quotation or a reference among dozens of PDFs and notes.

  • Managers

    Find a figure in an old presentation and open it on the right slide.

  • Teachers

    Find an exercise or an instruction in last year’s course materials.

  • Teams

    Search the shared drives as well as My Drive: meeting notes, reports, tracking sheets, wherever the word was written.

Your files stay yours

PeekDrive works in your browser. There is no PeekDrive server that could see your files or your searches.
