Find the exact passage, not just the file

With PeekDrive, go further than Google Drive’s built-in search. PeekDrive shows you exactly where to find what you are looking for, in each of your files.

  • Search inside the content

    Type a word or a phrase: PeekDrive looks for it in the text of your documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs.

  • Passages in their context

    For each file, you preview every occurrence of the word or phrase you searched for, along with the text around it, so you know the context. You know whether it is the right one before opening anything.

  • One click to the right spot

    Click “Open” to reach the right occurrence in a document, the right sheet and the right cell in a spreadsheet, the right slide in a presentation.

  • The files you use every day

    Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, PDF, Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint, CSV and plain text. In My Drive and in shared drives.

  • Four ways to save time

    The PeekDrive icon in Google Chrome’s extensions bar, the PeekDrive button next to Google Drive’s search bar and a line under Drive’s search suggestions or, for power users, the shortcut Cmd+Shift+F (Mac) / Ctrl+Shift+F (Windows) on Google Drive.

  • Private by design

    Read-only access, everything processed in your browser, no PeekDrive server, no AI. Your files only travel between your browser and Google.

How it works

Three steps, from Google Drive.

  1. 01

    Open PeekDrive

    Click the PeekDrive icon in Google Chrome’s extensions bar, or the PeekDrive button next to Google Drive’s search bar. Sign in with Google.

  2. 02

    Type a word or a phrase

    PeekDrive shows every occurrence in each file, with the text around it, so you know the context.

  3. 03

    Open at the right spot

    Click “Open” to land directly on the right occurrence. The document’s own search stays open on your word, so you can move from one occurrence to the next.

Why PeekDrive asks for access to your Google Drive

PeekDrive asks you to sign in with Google and grant read-only access to your Google Drive, so it can search the text inside your files and show you the matching passages. It cannot modify or delete your files.
