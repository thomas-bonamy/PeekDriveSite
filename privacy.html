Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What PeekDrive does

PeekDrive is a browser extension that lets you search inside your Google Drive files, preview the matching passages and open a file at the right spot.

Google data PeekDrive accesses

With the read-only access to Google Drive that you grant, PeekDrive reads:

  • The names, types, modification dates, IDs and links of your files, to list the results
  • The display name of each file’s owner, shown in the results
  • The names of parent folders and shared drives, to show where each file is located
  • The content of your files, downloaded on the fly into your browser to find and show the matching passage, and to confirm that a file really contains your search

This data is never saved, and it is never sent anywhere: it only travels between your browser and Google.

On Google Drive, while you type in Drive’s own search box, PeekDrive reads that text to show it in its “Search for "…" inside your Drive files” row, directly on the page. The text is passed to the PeekDrive panel only when you click that row, and is then kept in memory for the current browsing session only.

When you click “Open”, your search term (never the content of your files) is copied to your clipboard, so you can find it in the document. PeekDrive then looks for the passage in the page of the opened file, to scroll to it and highlight it; nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

No PeekDrive server

PeekDrive has no server. Your searches and your files only travel between your browser and Google, over encrypted HTTPS connections to Google’s official APIs. Everything else (finding the passages, building the previews) happens locally in your browser.

PeekDrive:

  • Does not send your data to any server other than Google’s
  • Does not use analytics, tracking or telemetry, and sets no cookies
  • Does not use artificial intelligence: no AI reads your documents
  • Does not sell or share any data with third parties, and no human reads your data
  • Does not use your data for advertising or to train AI models

When you install or uninstall PeekDrive, your browser opens a page of peekdrive.com (getting started, feedback). The extension sends that page nothing but your language.

The page opened after uninstalling offers an optional feedback form. If you choose to send it, we receive the reason you selected, your message, the language in which the page was displayed and, if you provide it, your e-mail address. If you give that address, a checkbox lets you agree to be contacted again to help improve PeekDrive; your choice is sent with the form. This information reaches us by e-mail at contact@peekdrive.com, sent by this website’s own hosting (OVH). It is used only to improve PeekDrive and to reply to you, and it is not linked to your Google account or to your files. To limit abuse of the form, the page also sends how long it stayed open before sending (checked, then discarded), and the script that receives the form temporarily keeps a shortened salted hash of the sender’s IP address with the time of sending, not the address itself. To have your message deleted, write to contact@peekdrive.com.

What is stored, and for how long

The content of your files is read on the fly and is never written to disk. To make repeated searches faster, short excerpts and the text of the last few files read stay in your browser’s memory while the PeekDrive panel is open; they are erased when you clear the search or close the panel. PeekDrive keeps no search history: only your last search is kept, in memory and for the current browsing session only, so it is still there when you reopen the panel. Your Google access token is kept in memory only and is never written to disk. The only things saved in your browser are your settings (search mode, language, sort order, show file location), the size and position of the PeekDrive panel, and the tips you have dismissed: no Google data.

Google API usage

PeekDrive requests read-only access to your Google Drive, in order to search your files and read their content. It cannot modify, move, share, create or delete anything in your Drive. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files are opened directly in your browser.

PeekDrive’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, PeekDrive does not use Google data for advertising, does not transfer it to data brokers or information resellers, does not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes, and no human reads it.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove PeekDrive’s access to your Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions, and uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions. Uninstalling erases your settings. PeekDrive keeps neither your files nor your searches on any server, so there is nothing of that kind to delete. For any question, or to have a message you sent us deleted, write to contact@peekdrive.com.
